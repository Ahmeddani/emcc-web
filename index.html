Who We Are

Rooted in faith, working toward a home of our own

EMCC is a home for Eritrean Muslim families across Central Ohio — gathering for Ramadan iftars, Eid celebrations, halaqas, and Qur'an programs, and supporting one another through life's milestones, all rooted in faith and Eritrean heritage.

We don't yet have a permanent building of our own. That's why our executive committee has launched a feasibility study and membership drive — working toward EMCC's first community center in Columbus.
