Welcome to EMCC

The Eritrean Muslims Community of Columbus (EMCC) is a community organization serving Eritrean Muslim families across Central Ohio. What began as informal gatherings among a handful of families has grown into an organized community with an executive committee, over 60 families, and a shared mission: preserving our Islamic faith and Eritrean heritage while supporting one another through everyday life.

Today, EMCC brings our community together for Ramadan iftars, Eid celebrations, and mutual support, right here in Columbus, Ohio.

A growing community, building its first permanent home

We don't yet have a permanent building of our own. Today, EMCC gathers for Ramadan iftars, Eid celebrations, and community life in rented spaces across Columbus, all rooted in faith and Eritrean heritage. Our executive committee has launched a feasibility study and membership drive to change that.
