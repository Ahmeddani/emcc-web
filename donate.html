Zelle / Bank Transfer

emccohio2020@gmail.com

Please include "Building Fund," "Membership Dues," or "Zakat/Sadaqah" in the memo so we can direct your gift correctly.

PayPal / Debit or Credit Card

Choose an amount, then pay securely with PayPal or a debit/credit card — no PayPal account required for card payments.

Payments are processed securely by PayPal. EMCC never sees or stores your card details.
