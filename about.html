About EMCC

Our mission, our story, our people

Serving the Eritrean Muslim community of Columbus with faith, unity, and service since our founding.

Our Mission

Preserving faith and heritage, building community

The Eritrean Muslims Community of Columbus (EMCC) exists to provide a spiritual and social home for Eritrean Muslim families in Central Ohio. We gather for Ramadan iftars and Eid celebrations, while supporting one another through life's milestones.

We don't yet have a permanent building of our own. Our gatherings currently take place in rented halls and borrowed spaces. That's why we're now working toward our long-held goal: a permanent community center that our families can truly call home.

Our Values

  • 🕌 Faith: centering our community around worship and the teachings of Islam.
  • 🌍 Heritage: celebrating and preserving Eritrean culture and language.
  • 🤝 Unity: welcoming every family with warmth and respect.
  • 📚 Education: growing Islamic knowledge and learning together as a community.
  • 💚 Service: supporting those in need within and beyond our community.
Our Journey

How EMCC came to be

1

A Growing Need

Eritrean Muslim families settling in Columbus came together for iftars, Eid, and mutual support, meeting in homes and rented spaces.

2

Formalizing Our Organization

As the community grew, EMCC was formally established with an executive committee to organize gatherings, dues, and community life.

3

Membership on the Rise

Our membership has grown roughly three-fold, currently standing at 60 families and climbing.

4

Launching the Community Center Project

On April 26, 2026, the executive committee proposed a feasibility study and membership drive to pursue our first permanent community center. Read the full project.
