Building Our Future Home

EMCC Community Center Project

A feasibility study and membership drive to secure the first permanent gathering space for Eritrean Muslim families in Columbus.

Introduction

Why we're doing this

EMCC families have long aspired to have our own community center, a place where we can gather during Ramadan for iftar, celebrate Eid, and come together for other social activities. Because of the size of our community and the financial reality our families face in carrying the high cost of securing and running such a center, this has not yet been possible.

At our scheduled community meeting on April 26, 2026, the executive committee introduced a proposal to revisit this idea and launch a feasibility study, to be conducted by a task force made up of experts from within our own membership.

Why Now?

A community that's ready to grow

The executive committee strongly feels that our membership has risen at least three-fold, with the potential to reach 100 families within the next 12 months. We believe that by inviting every Eritrean Muslim family and individual living in Columbus and the surrounding areas, we can reach that goal.

We're also exploring including families in nearby Ohio cities like Dayton, Cincinnati, Toledo, and Cleveland, many of whom have expressed interest in joining our organization on multiple occasions.
