Community Programs & Get Involved

We don't have a permanent building yet, but our community stays active. Here's what's happening now, and how you can help build what's next.

Current Activities

What EMCC offers today

Held in rented halls and borrowed spaces around Columbus while we work toward a permanent home.

🌙

Ramadan Iftars

Community-wide iftar gatherings throughout Ramadan, bringing families together to break their fast.

🎉

Eid Celebrations

Community Eid gatherings for Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, celebrated together as one family.

🧕

Youth & Family Events

Gatherings and activities designed to strengthen identity, friendship, and community bonds among our youth.

🕊️

Funeral & Janazah Support

Coordination and support for grieving families, including ghusl and janazah prayer arrangements.

💚

Community Assistance

Support for families facing hardship, connecting families with resources and mutual aid.

🌍

Cultural & Heritage Events

Gatherings that keep Eritrean traditions, language, and community spirit alive across generations.

💍

Nikah & Marriage Support

Guidance and coordination for nikah ceremonies rooted in Islamic tradition.

🏗️

Community Center Project

Our feasibility study and membership drive toward securing a permanent home. Learn more.

Volunteer

EMCC runs on community effort

From the targeted membership drive to the feasibility study task force, our organization is powered entirely by volunteers. No contribution is too small, whether you have an hour a month or a regular weekly commitment.
