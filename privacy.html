Privacy Policy

How EMCC collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

The Eritrean Muslims Community of Columbus ("EMCC", "we", "us") is a community organization in Columbus, Ohio. We collect only the personal information we need to serve our members, and we never sell it.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use, share and protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It covers our website (www.emccohio.org), our membership and dues records, donations, and EMCC's internal administration application.

2. Information we collect

SourceWhat we collect
Website contact formYour name, email address, subject and message, delivered to EMCC by email through our form provider, Formspree
MembershipName, membership ID, contact details, and dues and payment history
Donations by card or PayPalYour name, email and donation amount, received from PayPal. PayPal processes the payment; EMCC never sees or stores your card or bank details
Donations by ZelleYour name and the amount, as shown on EMCC's bank statement
EMCC bank deposits via PlaidDate, amount and description (which may include the payer's name) of deposits into EMCC's own bank account

About Plaid: EMCC administrators use Plaid Inc. to securely connect EMCC's own organization bank account to our administration application, so we can match incoming dues and donations to member records. Members never connect their personal bank accounts, and we never receive anyone's bank login credentials or account numbers. Plaid's handling of data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other sites.

3. How we use information

We use personal information only to run EMCC and serve our members:

  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To record and acknowledge dues and donations, and to match them with deposits into EMCC's bank account.
  • To keep accurate membership and financial records, including reports to our Executive Committee.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations, such as IRS recordkeeping for nonprofit organizations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only with the service providers below, only so they can provide their service to EMCC, or when the law requires it.

ProviderWhat it does for EMCC
PlaidConnects EMCC's own bank account to our administration application
PayPalProcesses card and PayPal donations
FormspreeDelivers website contact form messages to EMCC's email
SupabaseHosts our administration database
Google (Gmail)EMCC email, including login codes for administrators
Our bankHolds EMCC's account and processes Zelle transfers

Within EMCC, only approved officers and administrators can see member and financial records, and only for EMCC business.

5. How we protect information

We protect personal information with encryption, limited access and multi-factor authentication:

  • In transit: data sent between our systems and service providers is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • At rest: our database is encrypted, and sensitive credentials such as Plaid access tokens are additionally encrypted by our application.
  • Access: only approved EMCC officers and administrators have access, each with their own account, a password and a one-time code sent to their email.
  • Devices: the administration application is used only on EMCC-issued laptops with full-disk encryption, never on personal devices.
  • Maintenance: we keep our software and its components updated and monitored for security vulnerabilities.

Our practices follow EMCC's Access Control Policy and Data Retention and Disposal Policy.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed, then delete it securely:

InformationHow long we keep it
Dues, payment and donation records7 years after the end of the fiscal year, as required for nonprofit financial records
Member profileWhile you are a member, plus 7 years after your last payment; contact details removed on request when membership ends
Bank deposits received through Plaid that are not matched to a memberUntil resolved; then deleted after 24 months
EMCC's Plaid bank connectionWhile connected; removed within 30 days of disconnecting
Contact form messages2 years after our last reply

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, and we will respond within 30 days.

Email emccohio2020@gmail.com. We will confirm your identity using the contact details we already have before acting. We may need to keep some records, such as financial records we must keep by law; if so, we will tell you what we kept and why, and delete it when that period ends.

8. Children, changes and contact

Children: our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online.

Changes: we may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

Contact us: Eritrean Muslims Community of Columbus, Columbus, OH, emccohio2020@gmail.com.
