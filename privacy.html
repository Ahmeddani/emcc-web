Privacy Policy

How EMCC collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

The Eritrean Muslims Community of Columbus ("EMCC", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website (www.emccohio.org) and our internal administration application, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

From our website

  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject, and message. Submissions are delivered to EMCC by email through our form provider, Formspree.
  • Donations: card and PayPal donations are processed entirely by PayPal. EMCC never sees or stores your card or bank details. We receive your name, email, and donation amount from PayPal. Zelle transfers are handled by your bank and ours.

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other sites.

Membership and payment records

To manage membership and dues, EMCC keeps member records such as name, membership ID, contact details, and payment history.

Bank account data via Plaid

EMCC administrators use Plaid Inc. to securely connect EMCC's own organization bank account to our internal administration application. Through Plaid we receive information about deposits into that account, including the transaction date, amount, and description (which may include the payer's name). We use it only to match incoming dues and donations to member records. Members do not connect their personal bank accounts, and we never receive members' bank login credentials or account numbers.

Plaid's handling of data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To record and acknowledge dues and donations, and to reconcile them with our bank deposits.
  • To maintain accurate membership and financial records, including reports to our executive committee.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (such as Plaid, PayPal, Formspree, our database host, and our email provider), solely to provide their services to EMCC, or when required by law.

4. How we protect information

  • All data sent between our systems and service providers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Stored data is encrypted at rest, and sensitive credentials such as Plaid access tokens are additionally encrypted by our application.
  • Access to the administration application is limited to authorized EMCC administrators and requires a password plus a one-time code (multi-factor authentication).
  • We keep our software and dependencies updated and monitored for security vulnerabilities.

5. How long we keep information

We keep membership and financial records for as long as needed for membership administration and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete them. Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. If EMCC disconnects its bank account from Plaid, we stop receiving new bank data and delete the stored connection credentials.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to records we must keep by law. Email us at emccohio2020@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
